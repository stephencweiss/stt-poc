This was a complex TAVR case using a thirty-four millimeter Evolut platform. Because of the patient's low cardiac output, we placed an Impella for hemodynamic support during the valvuloplasty. Transfemoral access was large-bore, requiring a ProGlide closure. There was a small hematoma noted at the femoral artery, which was managed with manual pressure. Patient will remain on clopidogrel and aspirin. No signs of stroke or focal deficits post-procedure.
